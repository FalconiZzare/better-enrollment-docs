### Security

- **Medium**: a customised organization plugin `creatorRole` is now held to the same rule as `owner`: an invite grants it only with `allowOwnerInvites` and from a member who holds such a role.
- **Low**: an inviter who no longer holds an owner-level role cannot complete its earlier owner invites; they are withdrawn when its role changes, and re-checked at redemption with `revalidateInviterOnRedeem`.
- **Low**: `org-create` invites found an organization, so creating one, resending one as a public link and redeeming your own now need `invite: ["manage-orgs"]`. See [The invite actions](/docs/permissions#the-invite-actions).
- **Low**: a disabled organization stays closed when a request names it through an invitation id or a team.

### May affect you

- **Founding organizations**: access-control roles that create `org-create` invites with `invite: ["create"]` alone need `manage-orgs` added. Apps that gate invites with `adminRoles`, `adminUserIds` or `canManageInvites` are not affected.

### Migration notes

No schema changes and no new options.
